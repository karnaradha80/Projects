## User Request
Where are the gaps in the utility industry that can be filled by Data and AI?  
What is the main issue where we can start as an AI product?

---

## Key Findings

### 8 Major Gaps Identified
1. **Predictive Asset Failure** — transformers, cables, poles failing without warning
2. **Energy Theft / NTL** — 15–25% losses in India; $89B globally
3. **Outage Prediction & Restoration** — slow fault isolation, no pre-storm intelligence
4. **Demand Forecasting** — EVs + solar disrupting old regression models
5. **Vegetation Management** — 25–35% of outages from trees, manual inspection too slow
6. **Field Workforce Intelligence** — aging workforce, knowledge loss, scheduling inefficiency
7. **Customer Service & Billing** — 70% of calls are predictable + automated-able
8. **Regulatory & Compliance** — massive manual reporting burden

### Top Recommendation: Start with Predictive Asset Failure

**Why:**
- Every utility has the problem
- Data already exists (SCADA + GIS + work orders)
- ROI is direct (₹5–50 Lakhs per avoided transformer failure)
- PoC possible in 4–8 weeks on historical data
- Natural platform expansion: transformers → full Grid Intelligence Platform

### Product Evolution Path
1. Transformer Health Scoring (Month 1–6)
2. Add NTL/Theft Detection (Month 4–9) 
3. Add Outage Intelligence (Month 8–12)
4. → Full Grid Intelligence Platform

### Elevator Pitch
"We help electricity distribution utilities reduce unplanned outages by 60% and recover 5–10% of lost revenue using AI on data they already have."
