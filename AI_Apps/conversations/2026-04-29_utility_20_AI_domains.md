# Conversation: Utility Industry — Expanded to 20 AI Domains
**Date:** 2026-04-29  
**Project:** AI_Apps

---

## User Request
Expand the 8 AI gaps by adding GIS, Service Desk, Asset Management, Application Maintenance, and similar enterprise-wide domains.

---

## Outcome

Expanded from 8 → **20 AI domains** across the full utility enterprise.

### New Domains Added (12 new beyond original 8)
| # | Domain | Key AI Use Case |
|---|--------|----------------|
| 9 | GIS Intelligence | Auto-update from field, infrastructure extraction from imagery, spatial risk analysis |
| 10 | Asset Management (EAM) | Data enrichment, repair vs replace AI, spare parts optimization, failure mode library |
| 11 | IT Service Desk AI | Intelligent ticket routing, L1 auto-resolution, proactive incident detection |
| 12 | AIOps / App Maintenance | Legacy code documentation, log intelligence, root cause analysis, change impact analysis |
| 13 | Renewable / DER Management | VPP orchestration, EV load management, solar/wind forecasting |
| 14 | Safety / HSE AI | Near-miss pattern analysis, arc flash risk, PPE compliance via computer vision |
| 15 | Procurement / Supply Chain | Spare parts demand forecasting, vendor risk scoring, contract intelligence |
| 16 | HR & Workforce AI | Skill gap analysis, knowledge transfer capture, attrition prediction |
| 17 | OT/IT Cybersecurity | OT anomaly detection, IT/OT threat correlation |
| 18 | MDM Intelligence | Missing read imputation, anomalous read detection, AMI network health |
| 19 | Network Planning AI | Load growth modeling, EV infrastructure planning, capital prioritization |
| 20 | Sustainability / ESG | Carbon footprint aggregation, SF6 leak detection, ESG report generation |

### Key Insight: GIS + Asset Management are PREREQUISITES
- Bad GIS → wrong asset locations → AI predictions assigned to wrong assets
- Bad EAM data → missing asset age → failure prediction accuracy drops 30–40%
- Fix the data foundation first, then all other AI use cases perform better

### Priority Order
- **P1 (Foundation):** GIS Intelligence, Asset Management AI, Asset Failure Prediction, NTL Detection
- **P2 (Quick Wins):** Outage Intelligence, IT Service Desk AI, AIOps, Customer Service AI, MDM
- **P3 (Scale):** Procurement, Demand Forecasting, DER, Safety, Field Workforce, Network Planning
- **P4 (Strategic):** Vegetation, HR, Regulatory, Cybersecurity, ESG
